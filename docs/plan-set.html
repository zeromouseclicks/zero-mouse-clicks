Home / The plan set

Eight sheets.
One PDF.

From one floor plan image, Zero Mouse Clicks draws the plan set an architect would: an A3 PDF with eight sheets, about 200 KB, ready to quote a remodel, plan furniture or start a CAD drawing.

Every sheet has an ISO 5457 frame with grid references and a title column: brand, a panel for that sheet, key plan, general notes, revisions, and a title block with a large sheet number. Each view has a title and a scale bar, uses the largest standard scale that fits (such as 1:50), and the free space carries schedules and quantities.

G-001 Cover and index

Key figures (net area, footprint, spaces, doors and windows), the source image, the sheet index, the list of spaces, and what was read, measured or assumed.

A-101 Floor plan

Walls, doors with their swing, windows, axes with bubbles, chained and overall dimensions, room names and areas, fixed casework and fixtures drawn as standard blocks, with a legend and a room schedule.

A-102 Schedules

Area and finish schedule with share bars and an area diagram, door and window schedule with schematic type elevations, counts of fixtures, casework and furniture, and the assumptions used.

A-103 Floor finishes

The finish of each room read from the image texture (wood, tile, porcelain, exterior), with swatches and quantities per finish: area in m² and perimeter.

A-104 Electrical (schematic)

A rule-based proposal of light points, outlets, GFCI outlets and switches, with quantities per room. It is not read from the source plan.

A-105 Furniture layout

Furniture and fixtures read from the image (bed, nightstand, sofa, armchair, dining table and chairs, desk, TV unit, stools, plants, appliances, sanitary fixtures), redrawn as parametric blocks at catalogue sizes and facing the right way. Indicative.

A-201 Section through the kitchen

A cut through the main counter (base and wall cabinets, hood, fridge; heights assumed) and an enlarged plan of the kitchen with fixture sizes and countertop length.

A-202 Section through the bathroom

A cut through the largest bathroom (fixtures, tiling, mirror; heights assumed) and an enlarged plan with fixture sizes.

What it is good for

  • Quoting a remodel or a kitchen before you have the keys.
  • Planning furniture with real sizes instead of a picture.
  • Comparing units of a development with the same drawing standard.
  • A clean starting point for a CAD or BIM model.

What it is not

It is a drawing made from a marketing image, not a measured survey. Sections, heights and the electrical layout are assumptions. Verify dimensions on site, and have a professional review the drawings before using them for permits, fabrication or construction.

Need CAD or something else?

The site gives a PDF. For an editable CAD file, more sections or a quote, write to a human.
